### "Why doesn't video work?"
*"WebRTC needs TURN servers for strict networks - about $50/month. We're using free STUN servers which work on most networks but not all. For this demo we're showing the interface in simulation mode. The tech is implemented - just needs production infrastructure."*

### "Is this just ChatGPT?"
*"No - it's a rules engine plus optional OpenAI. Rules detect life events in under 1 second with 100% accuracy. OpenAI just polishes the wording. The app works fully without any paid APIs."*

### "How does it scale?"
*"Serverless architecture auto-scales. Current free tier: 100 users. For 1000+ we'd need load testing (2-3 days) and paid tier ($20/month). Same pattern Netflix uses."*

### "What about security?"
*"bcrypt passwords, parameterized queries, HTTPS everywhere, audit logging. For production we'd do a security audit ($500-1000) before handling real data. Standard practice."*

### "Have you tested with users?"
*"Internal testing only - next step is beta with 5-10 reps for 2-3 months. We know we can't ship to thousands on day one without real user feedback."*

### "I don't understand [technical thing]"
